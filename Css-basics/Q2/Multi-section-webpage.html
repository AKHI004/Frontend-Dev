Overview

Kyoto, once the capital of Japan, is known for its timeless beauty, ancient temples, and traditional culture. It beautifully blends modern living with centuries-old traditions, making it one of the most captivating cities in the world.

Cultural Heritage

From golden temples to tea ceremonies, Kyoto reflects Japan’s deep respect for art and spirituality. The Kinkaku-ji Temple, Gion district, and hundreds of shrines make Kyoto a living museum of Japanese history.

Nature & Serenity

Cherry blossoms in spring and crimson leaves in autumn transform Kyoto into a painter’s dream. The Arashiyama Bamboo Grove offers peace and a surreal experience, attracting visitors from around the world.

Travel Information

Kyoto is well-connected via the Shinkansen (bullet train) and offers various accommodation options from traditional ryokans to luxury hotels. The best time to visit is between March and May or October and November.
